About

Founded in N’Djamena in 2019.

SOTGEC-BTP works in studies, building construction and renovation, public works, water supply and sanitation. The group has since expanded into real estate and consulting, and has an office in Abidjan.

What guides our work.

Six principles, on every project.

Rigour

Careful studies, detailed pricing, close control of time and cost.

Transparency

A written scope, progress reviews, a final report.

Safety

Risk prevention is part of the project from day one.

Local roots

Teams and partners on the ground.

Independent advice

Consulting is kept separate from construction.

Commitments in writing

Only a written, signed proposal binds SOTGEC.
